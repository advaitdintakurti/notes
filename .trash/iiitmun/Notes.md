
## Alliances

-> **European Union (EU):** As a founding member, Germany is deeply integrated into the EU's political and economic frameworks, advocating for collective European interests.

-> **NATO:** Germany is a key member of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization, emphasizing transatlantic security cooperation.

-> **Weimar Triangle:** Established in 1991, this alliance between Germany, France, and Poland aims to promote cooperation on European issues.

-> **United States:** Despite occasional policy differences, the U.S. remains one of Germany's closest and most important partners outside Europe.



-> Germany advocates for a multilateral global order based on democratic values, human rights, and the rule of law.


### Policies on Weapons of Mass Destruction (WMDs)

Germany is committed to non-proliferation and disarmament.
-> **Nuclear Weapons:** Germany does not possess nuclear weapons and is a signatory to the Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons (NPT). It supports global disarmament initiatives and has been involved in negotiations to manage nuclear waste responsibly.
    
-> **Chemical and Biological Weapons:** Germany is a party to the Chemical Weapons Convention (CWC) and the Biological Weapons Convention (BWC), advocating for the elimination of such weapons globally.

However, Germany is a member of NATO and participates in nuclear sharing arrangements, which involve hosting U.S. nuclear weapons on its territory. Approximately 60 tactical B61 nuclear bombs are stored in Germany, and German forces train to deliver these weapons in the event of conflict


## Germany's Policies on Privatization

Germany employs a mixed economy model, balancing private enterprise with public welfare:
-> **Economic Policy:** While supporting free-market principles, Germany maintains significant public sector involvement in essential services and infrastructure. 
-> **Privatization Stance:** Germany approaches privatization cautiously, ensuring that it does not compromise public interests or lead to monopolies.

**Emphasize Ethical Considerations:** Highlight the importance of ethical standards in the creation and deployment of superhumans, drawing parallels to Germany's commitment to human rights and the responsible use of technology.

**Support Non-Proliferation Principles:** Argue against the unrestricted national development of superhumans, citing concerns over a potential arms race and the destabilization of global security, similar to WMD proliferation issues.

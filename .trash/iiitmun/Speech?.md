## Committee Debate & Formal Discussion

### Strategies for Effective Debate Participation

1. **Position Reinforcement:**
    - Reiterate Germany’s commitment to multilateralism by stressing historical alliances (EU, NATO, Weimar Triangle) and a legacy of balanced diplomacy.
    - Highlight real-world parallels with nuclear non-proliferation and arms control agreements.
2. **Structured Questions & Moderated Caucuses:**
    - Pose inquiries regarding accountability measures:  
        • “How will we ensure that an international oversight body is immune to political manipulation?”  
        • “What mechanisms will verify compliance with ethical standards in the deployment of superhumans?”
    - Encourage debate on the potential risks of privatized superhuman assets leading to inequitable power distribution.
3. **Coalition Building:**
    - Identify potential allies among EU and NATO members; propose joint working groups or drafting committees.
    - Engage in bilateral meetings during unmoderated caucuses to develop consensus on key clauses.

### Supplementary Talking Points for Debate

- **Ethical Imperatives:** “Can technology truly be ethical without robust oversight? How do we prevent engineered forces from becoming tools of oppression?”
- **Equity in Access:** “What safeguards are necessary to ensure that even nations with limited resources can access these assets for humanitarian and defensive purposes?”
- **International Precedents:** “Let us draw on the successes and failures of historical disarmament treaties to craft a framework that is both flexible and enforceable.”

## Draft Resolution Negotiation

### Constructing a Robust Resolution

**Proposed Resolution Structure:**

4. **Article I – Establishment of an International Oversight Committee (IOC):**
    - Mandate: Supervise the ethical creation, distribution, and deployment of superhumans.
    - Structure: Representatives from all UN member states, with special seats for key stakeholders (EU, NATO, etc.).
    - Functions: Regular audits, compliance verification, and dispute resolution mechanisms.
    
5. **Article II – Ethical Standards & Transparency Measures:**
    - Define strict ethical guidelines that mirror existing disarmament protocols.
    - Require public reporting on superhuman production, deployment, and financial transactions to prevent corruption and undue influence.
    - Establish an independent review board for ethical assessments.
    
6. **Article III – Equitable Distribution & National Safeguards:**
    - Introduce criteria ensuring that superhuman assets are allocated based on need, humanitarian considerations, and national security requirements.
    - Include provisions for emergency aid to nations at risk of being marginalized.
    - Enforce a cap on exclusive long-term contracts that might monopolize resources.
    
7. **Article IV – Non-Proliferation & Disarmament Protocols:**
    - Draw parallels with treaties like the NPT, ensuring that no nation can independently develop a monopoly of superhuman assets.
    - Introduce verification measures, periodic inspections, and sanctions for non-compliance.
    
8. **Article V – Public-Private Partnership & Accountability:**
    - Outline collaborative frameworks between governments and private entities like DASA.
    - Set benchmarks for transparency, profit reinvestment into public welfare, and community engagement.

### B. Negotiation Tactics & Strategic Flexibility

- **Core Principles vs. Negotiable Details:**  
    • Stand firm on the need for multilateral oversight and ethical guidelines.  
    • Be flexible with specific language on economic clauses or technical oversight details.
- **Coalition Leverage:**  
    • Utilize Germany’s diplomatic credibility to encourage alliances with key EU and NATO delegates.  
    • Propose joint statements or working papers that reinforce shared principles.
- **Anticipating Objections:**
